Tilecochet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Tilecochet is a puzzle game for Android published by the developer account byadyge. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not request internet permission. It does not send any data from your device.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, star scores and sound setting are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a later version adds them, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
