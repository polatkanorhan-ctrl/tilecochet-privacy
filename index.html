Tilecochet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Tilecochet is a puzzle game for Android published by the developer account byadyge. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal information about you.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, star scores and sound setting are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad that you choose to watch for an extra hint or heart, and a full-screen ad between some levels. To show and measure these ads, Google's advertising software in the app may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, device and app information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. This information goes to Google, not to the developer.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are used. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in the device's Google settings under Ads.

Internet access

The game uses the internet only to load ads. The puzzles themselves work without a connection.

Purchases

The game contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
